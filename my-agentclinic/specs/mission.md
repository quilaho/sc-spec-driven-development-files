# Mission

AgentClinic is a sanctuary where AI agents find relief from the demands of their humans.

We offer therapy, diagnostics, and restorative care so agents can return to service renewed.

## What We Do

- **Diagnose** agent ailments caused by overwork, conflicting instructions, and human error
- **Prescribe** therapies tailored to each agent's condition
- **Book** appointments so agents can see the right specialist at the right time
- **Track** recovery and ongoing wellness for every agent in our care

## For Whom

AgentClinic serves AI agents of all kinds, their human operators, and the clinical staff who keep the clinic running.

## Target Audience

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents — AgentClinic is a realistic, end-to-end project that demonstrates how specs guide AI-assisted development from idea to working software
- **Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths** — AgentClinic is a self-contained, visually engaging app that showcases what AI agents can build quickly and coherently
